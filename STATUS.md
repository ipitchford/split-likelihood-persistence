# Status

- **Version:** 0.1.0-candidate, 1 November 2026. Tag `v0.1.0-candidate`, which will never be moved.
- **Status:** unrefereed research candidate. Internal AI-assisted review only; not peer-reviewed, independently reproduced or formally verified.
- **DOI:** 10.5281/zenodo.23856728 (concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23856727).
- **Question:** Pace, Salvan and Sartori (Canadian Journal of Statistics 51, 2023, Section 3) conjectured that the split likelihood-ratio ("split-naive") universal-inference set, recomputed after every new pair of observations with the fixed threshold log(1/epsilon), has asymptotic persistence at least 1 - epsilon.
- **Answer:** no. The asymptotic persistence is 0.
- **Proved in the manuscript (written proofs):** Proposition 0 (zero persistence in any model with a permutation-symmetric training estimator whenever the fixed-n non-coverage does not vanish; Hewitt-Savage zero-one law); Lemma 1 (vector LIL for continuous 2-homogeneous functionals); Theorem 1 (Gaussian two-stream scheme: log E_n = (S_n T_n - T_n^2/2)/n exactly, limsup / log log n = (sqrt5 - 1)/2 a.s., tail intersection empty a.s.); Theorem 2 (same constant in every regular model satisfying the information identity; sandwich constant ((sqrt5 - 1)/2) mu_max(H^-1 J) otherwise); Theorem 3 (split ratio r gives c(r) = (sqrt(r^2 + 4r) - r)/2); Theorem 4 (fixed-K cross-fitting: c(1/(K-1)) for weighted power means with p > 0, 1/K for equal-weight geometric, harmonic and minimum aggregation, bounds for symmetric e-merging functions); Theorem 5 (threshold lambda_1 g(n)^2/n gives a genuine confidence sequence for any valid scalar Gaussian boundary g); Theorem 6 (sharpness: thresholds lambda_1{2 log log n + kappa log log log n + O(1)} achievable iff kappa > 3); Proposition 5A (model-free checkpoint repair); Proposition 7 (exact Ornstein-Uhlenbeck representation of the finite-horizon uncoverage).
- **Computed, not proved:** the OU failure exponents (0.0421 at 80%, 0.0054 at 95%, 0.0005 at 99%), finite-horizon uncoverages, the horizons at which the error first exceeds nominal (about 105 times the first look at 80%, 10^3.9 at 95%, 10^8.8 at 99%, the last by power-law extrapolation) and the horizon-calibrated thresholds (Tables 1 and 3; numerical, with stated empirical error estimates); seeded Monte Carlo reproductions of Pace et al.'s Gaussian Tables 2-3 and of their Poisson example; the finite-looks table (Table 4); efficiency comparisons (Table 5).
- **Not claimed:** anything about fixed-n universal inference (valid), the running-MLE ("split-exact") construction (valid), Pace et al.'s mixture confidence sequences or their separate Section 6 conjecture; rate constants for irregular models; growing-K or re-randomised cross-fitting; a plug-in sandwich repair for composite likelihoods; a resolution of the Poisson discrepancy with Pace et al.'s Table 5 (it also affects their non-split mixture row; its source is unresolved).
